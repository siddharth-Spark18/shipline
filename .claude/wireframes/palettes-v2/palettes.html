Palette options

Classic stays. The five new palettes have quiet near-neutral backgrounds, a light sidebar (not a coloured one) and one muted accent. Each mock shows the sidebar with its selected item, the Setup header buttons, summary cards, guide/warn/block badges and an input. Every text and badge colour here is at least 4.5:1 contrast, in light and dark.
