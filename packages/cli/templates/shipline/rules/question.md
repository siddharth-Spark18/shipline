# Question rules

<!-- Repo-specific instructions Claude must follow in the "question" stage. Filled in by /shipline-setup; edit freely. -->

- Write `question.md` with a `## Question` heading and a `## Success criteria` heading (the stage can't finish without them, each with real text under it).
- State the question in one or two sentences, so that someone who was not in the conversation could answer it.
- Under Success criteria, say what a good answer looks like and how you will know the question is answered. Include a time or effort limit if the developer gave one.
- Say what is out of scope. A spike answers one question; list the follow-up questions you will not chase.
- Ask the developer when the question is vague. Don't guess what they want to learn.
- Don't change code in this workflow. The outcome is knowledge, not a change.
